What else a contractor usually needs for a firm quote

  • Structural drawings from an engineer: foundation or column footing, beams and slab reinforcement. Soil type affects foundation cost.
  • Elevation finishes: wall cladding, paint, and whether the roof stays flat or gets a sloped truss with tiles (common in Kerala).
  • Specification list: door and window material (teak, wood, steel, UPVC), tile size and rate per sq ft, sanitary fittings, kitchen counter.
  • Electrical points and plumbing layout, plus water tank, septic tank and well or borewell positions.
  • Site details: plot size, road access, compound wall and gate, if they are part of the job.
